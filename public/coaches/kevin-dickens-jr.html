Kevin Dickens Jr.

Five-Time Flag Football All-American · National Champion · Athlete Developer

Kevin Dickens Jr. brings more than two decades of competitive flag football experience and a deep understanding of the modern flag game. A former collegiate track and field athlete at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, Kevin became a five-time Flag Football All-American and a collegiate flag football national champion.

Beyond his playing accomplishments, Kevin has built and led competitive flag football programs, coached athletes, organized high-level competition, and helped create development pathways for youth, women's, and adult players.

He brings F3 athletes a combination of flag-specific intelligence, movement expertise, competitive experience, and an ability to translate the game into practical, position-specific instruction.

Areas of emphasis

  • Wide receiver development
  • Route running
  • Releases & separation
  • Speed & movement efficiency

What athletes learn from Kevin

Athletes working with Kevin sharpen the releases, route detail, and separation skills that define modern flag receiving, along with the speed and movement efficiency that let them win one-on-one — taught by someone who has competed and won at the sport's highest amateur levels.
